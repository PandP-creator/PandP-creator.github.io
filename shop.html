How to use your digital products

Fillable PDF journals

Prayer Journey Journal (Lavender and Soft Cream)

  1. Download the PDF to your phone, tablet or computer.
  2. Open it in a free PDF reader such as Adobe Acrobat Reader. Some browsers and phone viewers cannot save typed text, so a PDF reader app works best.
  3. Tap or click a box and type your prayers, dates, Scripture and answered prayers.
  4. Save often, and keep a backup copy by emailing it to yourself or saving it to cloud storage.
  5. Prefer pen and paper? Print the pages for your own personal use and write by hand.
  6. Your journal is personal, so please keep the file private and do not share it.

PDF eBooks and workbook

Bible Study Workbook, Breaking Free, Intentional Prayer

  1. Download the PDF and open it in any PDF reader, such as Adobe Acrobat Reader, your browser or your phone's built-in viewer.
  2. Read at your own pace. Zoom in if you want larger text.
  3. For the Bible Study Workbook, follow the 6-week plan one day at a time with your Bible and a notebook.
  4. For Breaking Free, take the 21 days one day at a time, and reach out to a pastor, counsellor or trusted person if you need support.
  5. For Intentional Prayer, read a section, then put it into practice in your daily prayer time.
  6. Files are for personal use. See our Terms before printing or sharing.
