Digital Product Refund Policy

Prayer & Purpose

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Introduction

Thank you for supporting Prayer & Purpose.

We create digital resources to support your prayer life, spiritual growth, and personal development. This policy explains how refunds, cancellations, and product-related problems are handled when you purchase our digital products.

By placing an order, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review the product description and this policy, subject to your rights under applicable law.

2. Products Covered

This policy applies to digital products offered by Prayer & Purpose, which may include:

  • Digital prayer journals.
  • Christian e-books and devotionals.
  • Printable prayer cards and worksheets.
  • Bible study guides.
  • Downloadable spiritual growth resources.
  • Other digital materials delivered electronically.

3. Digital Product Delivery

Digital products are generally delivered through a download link, email, or a third-party digital delivery platform after payment has been confirmed.

Delivery times may depend on the payment processor or delivery service used.

Please ensure that the email address you provide is correct and that your device supports the product's stated file format.

4. General Refund Policy

Because digital products can often be accessed, copied, or downloaded immediately, we generally do not offer refunds simply because a customer changes their mind after receiving or accessing a digital product.

However, this general policy does not remove or limit any consumer rights or refund entitlements that apply under the law.

We will consider refund or other appropriate remedies in situations such as:

  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • You paid but did not receive the product and we cannot resolve the delivery issue.
  • The download file is corrupted or inaccessible and we cannot provide a working replacement.
  • The product materially differs from its description.
  • A transaction was unauthorized or fraudulent, subject to appropriate verification.
  • A refund or other remedy is required by applicable law.

5. Incorrect or Damaged Downloads

If a download is missing, corrupted, incomplete, or does not open as described, please contact us.

We may first attempt to resolve the issue by sending a replacement file, correcting the download link, or providing technical assistance.

If the issue cannot reasonably be resolved, we will consider an appropriate refund or other remedy in accordance with applicable law.

6. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged twice for the same product, contact us with your order details or transaction references.

Once the duplicate payment is verified, we will arrange an appropriate refund for the duplicate charge.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request assistance or a refund, email us at:

Email: prayerandpurpose40@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Your name.
  • The email address used for the purchase.
  • The name of the product.
  • Your order number or payment reference, if available.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Relevant supporting information, where appropriate.

Please do not send your full payment card number, password, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Processing Refund Requests

We aim to acknowledge refund enquiries within five business days and communicate the next steps as soon as reasonably possible.

If a refund is approved, the time required for the money to reach you may depend on the payment provider, bank, or payment method used.

Where applicable law specifies a deadline or remedy, that requirement takes precedence over this general procedure.

9. Subscription Products

If Prayer & Purpose introduces subscriptions or recurring digital services, cancellation and refund terms will be disclosed separately before purchase.

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically entitle a customer to a refund for a completed billing period, except where required by applicable law or the specific subscription terms.

10. Third-Party Purchases

If you purchase a Prayer & Purpose product through a third-party marketplace or storefront, the marketplace's procedures may apply to payment processing and refund administration.

We will provide reasonable assistance where possible, but you may need to submit your request through the platform where you completed the purchase.

11. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict, or waive any rights that cannot legally be excluded or restricted under applicable consumer protection or other laws.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our products, delivery methods, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on our website.
