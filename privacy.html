Privacy Policy

Prayer & Purpose

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Introduction

Prayer & Purpose is a Christian digital-resource platform dedicated to supporting prayer, spiritual growth, and personal development through prayer journals, e-books, devotionals, downloadable guides, and related resources.

We respect your privacy and aim to handle your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to visitors and customers using our website and related digital services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features available on our website, we may collect:

Information you provide

  • Your name and email address when you contact us or subscribe to updates.
  • Information you provide when placing an order or requesting customer support.
  • Order details, such as the digital product purchased and transaction reference.
  • Messages you voluntarily send to us.

Technical information

  • IP address and technical information recorded by our hosting provider.
  • Browser type, device information, and website usage information where available.
  • Cookie preferences and other settings stored in your browser.

We only intend to collect information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and customer support requests.
  • Deliver purchased digital products.
  • Send order confirmations and essential service communications.
  • Send newsletters or promotional updates where appropriate consent or another lawful basis exists.
  • Maintain website security and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Understand website performance and improve our content, where analytics are enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Payments and Digital Products

Prayer & Purpose may use third-party payment processors, online storefronts, or digital delivery services to sell and deliver products.

Payment information is handled according to the relevant provider's systems and policies. We do not intend to store complete payment card details on our GitHub Pages website.

Please review the privacy policy of any payment or delivery provider you use.

5. Hosting and Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub documents that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes.

We may also use third-party services for email subscriptions, analytics, embedded media, payments, or digital product delivery if these features are added to our website.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual arrangements.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use essential technologies and, where configured, optional analytics or marketing technologies.

Where required, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies. You may change your preferences through the cookie settings available on our website.

For further details, please read our Cookie Policy.

7. Email Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we may use your email address to send updates about new prayer resources, devotionals, journals, and other Prayer & Purpose products.

You can unsubscribe from promotional emails using the unsubscribe link provided or by contacting us.

We may still send necessary communications relating to purchases, transactions, or customer service.

8. Prayer Requests and Sensitive Information

Prayer & Purpose is a faith-based platform. If we offer prayer request forms or receive messages about personal circumstances, please share only information you are comfortable providing.

Religious beliefs and some information that people may include in prayer requests can qualify as sensitive personal data under applicable law. We will handle any such information in accordance with applicable legal requirements and the purpose for which it was provided.

Please do not submit information about other people without appropriate permission.

9. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

However, no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We encourage you to avoid sending payment credentials, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary website forms or email.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including fulfilling orders, providing support, maintaining records, and meeting legal obligations.

Where information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal requirements and the capabilities of our service providers.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally applicable.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing communications.
  • Raise a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

12. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers may process information outside Kenya. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we aim to ensure that applicable legal requirements and safeguards are followed.

13. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences. If we provide children's resources, purchases and submissions should be handled by a parent or guardian where appropriate.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information beyond what is permitted by applicable law and necessary for the relevant service.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
